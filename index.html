STUDIO KOLPIXEL / ABOUT

Made by one creator.
Built one project at a time.

The Kolpixel Archive is a personal creative studio and learning project: a place to practise writing, web development, visual storytelling, historical research and strategic game design. It is meant to grow with its creator.

The archive is an independent work in progress. Project concepts and research notes are not all finished or publication-ready.
